# Architecture and correctness contract

The collector is read-only. The current exchange adapter uses Polymarket's public market-data services. An outcome/asset ID is the symbol; both outcomes of every eligible discovered market are collected. IDs remain strings (never narrowed to a machine integer).

`Gamma discovery -> stable shards x independent WebSockets -> bounded channel -> wire adapter -> single-owner replica books -> SBE encoders / aligned writer`

There is no frontend. CLI inspection and stderr health records support debugging.

## State and ordering

Persistent asset IDs share interned `Arc<str>` allocations; hot-path lookups borrow strings rather than acquiring interner locks. The sharded registry is pruned at catalog boundaries; process exit releases all storage and library callers can prune after dropping their owners. Its size follows live owners and retained resolution tombstones, not a constant bound. Catalog containers share ownership rather than duplicating every ID string. Typed metadata visitors skip unused fields.

Each connection has its own book per symbol, initialized only by a complete `book` message. `price_change` quantities are absolute quantities. Zero removes that price level. Prices and sizes parse directly from decimal strings into the schema's millionths, with checked integer arithmetic; excess nonzero precision is rejected rather than rounded. A frame is decoded/validated in full before any events from it reach the state machine. Within a frame, exchange order is retained. Bids serialize descending and asks ascending.

A symbol has one selected source. Standby updates modify only that source's independent book. On disconnect, that source's books are discarded. A healthy standby at or beyond the last emitted exchange timestamp can be promoted; its complete snapshot is emitted before later deltas. A standby behind that watermark cannot be promoted. Reconnected sources must deliver a fresh snapshot before any delta is accepted. A new snapshot replaces both sides completely. Resolved symbols are tombstoned so stale discovery cannot resurrect them.

Deltas received before the first full book are buffered without advancing a source watermark: at most 256 per asset and 4 MiB of conservatively accounted storage per socket. After a book arrives, buffered changes at or after its timestamp replay in stable timestamp/arrival order; older changes are already incorporated. Overflow disconnects that source before partial output rather than silently dropping continuity. Only validated snapshots and book changes feed the monotonic activity monitor; trades and best-bid/ask traffic cannot conceal an unsynchronized book. Silence does not invalidate a healthy book. Silence/rate/initialization recovery refreshes a bounded group of assets on the same socket. The refreshed replica remains usable while its request waits for permission/budget. After the budget grant, it resets immediately before the actual unsubscribe write and suspends that asset until restore, then requires a fresh snapshot before replaying buffered/new deltas. A standby can promote; single-primary operation exposes temporary missing coverage. Restore bypasses the PONG pause while retaining both rate budgets. Rotation opens a warm replacement while the old connection continues reading. Once replacement snapshots cover previously initialized assets and satisfy timestamp watermarks, the old socket is closed. Replacements are serialized per shard and capped at four globally. Rotation is staggered across replicas, selects the most overdue slot, and restarts its interval after reconnect/ramp completion. Replacement gates capture the outgoing source's currently initialized watermarks once at ramp completion; resets and removals prune the target. The engine independently enforces its emitted watermark when promoting. A shared FIFO subscription budget and an application-PONG progress barrier limit initialization/maintenance pressure while socket reads continue. Parked tickets retain seniority and release unused grants; eligibility skips parked entries, and reserved grants cannot be stolen by a resuming predecessor. A failed or timed-out warmup keeps the old healthy socket. Snapshot grace begins after actual subscription completion; a separate scaled ramp deadline bounds incomplete initialization. Truly disconnected sources are invalidated in order.

Completed catalog updates and lifecycle additions retain shared Asset handles through the coordinator; they do not rebuild every identifier as an owned String. Market additions/removals flow through a watch channel into existing sessions, using paced subscribe/unsubscribe frames without restarting a shard. The engine removes symbols before later socket updates can reintroduce them. Physical connection IDs are unique across reconnect generations.

Unchanged absolute updates are idempotent no-ops. Equal timestamps remain legal and preserve stream order (multiple updates may share a millisecond). Every complete wire frame is validated inside its source task before forwarding. A malformed known frame closes that source and reconnects with fresh snapshots. Strictly older deltas covered by its full snapshot are dropped. Other per-asset regressions quarantine and reset that asset before a targeted resnapshot, preserving unrelated books. Unknown extension event types are ignored without book mutation. Other shards and healthy replicas continue. The coordinator retains terminal checks for impossible/internally inconsistent input and output I/O errors. The engine is transport-independent; only Polymarket is implemented, not a speculative Kalshi adapter.

Polymarket's documented public raw feed has no contiguous sequence number. Timestamps and book hashes do not prove that every update arrived, nor do equal timestamps disambiguate replayed conflicting messages. This collector cannot promise gap-free capture, exactly-once delivery, or reconstruction of missed intermediate deltas. Redundancy and resnapshots improve recovery; they do not recover history that neither socket received. Hash checksum validation is not implemented because this submission does not establish a compatible documented checksum algorithm.

## Snapshots, output and lifecycle

The live coordinator streams complete snapshots every 60 seconds from process start for initialized selected books whose source remains connected. It uses a skip policy after scheduling stalls, avoiding bursts of falsely historical snapshots. No empty placeholder is emitted for a book that has never synchronized. Minute diagnostics include universe, emitted, missing and duration; resnapshots compare exact levels only when exchange timestamps match. These are diagnostics, not a feed checksum guarantee. The health record reports discovered versus ready symbols so missing coverage is visible. Replay uses a virtual minute clock, explicitly labeled synthetic evidence.

The upstream `SbeEncode` implementation writes binary SBE messages to stdout. Human-readable messages go only to stderr. `--output PATH` also uses the unchanged `AlignedSbeWriter`; it pads 64 MiB chunks to be readable by the unchanged `AlignedSbeReader`. Stdout is flushed each minute; the aligned file writes full chunks automatically and flushes its final partial chunk only at shutdown, avoiding 64 MiB padding per minute. `--no-stdout` enables file-only operation. Stdout is the stream interface; the aligned format supports persisted files. `inspect` produces diagnostic JSONL from an aligned file. File paths must be new; existing files are rejected.

SIGINT cancels discovery and socket producers, closes connections, joins owned tasks, drains the bounded channel's accepted messages, then flushes and drops the output handles. Output errors and fatal discovery/connection failures cancel ingestion and return a nonzero status; recoverable wire or per-asset ordering faults are scoped as described above. A file write/flush error poisons and discards the supplied writer so its retained chunk is never retried after a partial write. Stdout buffer failure also prevents implicit drop-flush retries. Complete chunks before an I/O failure remain valid, but the final chunk can be truncated; `inspect` rejects an incomplete aligned file. File flush means written to the operating system, not fsync/power-loss durability. A slow or blocked OS output sink may delay shutdown; this is not a hard real-time system. The binary stream must have a consumer, not an unread pipe.

## Operational boundaries

The input queue holds at most 64 events; WebSocket frames/messages are limited to 8 MiB, discovery pages to 32 MiB and base live connections to 128, plus at most one warm socket per shard and four globally. Explicit ineligibility excludes a market. Valid nullable flags produce provisional candidates, while malformed records are quarantined and retain previous market assets. Canonical IDs are selected by protocol version; missing IDs retain previous subscriptions by market ID and are reported unresolved. Metadata uncertainty is not labeled complete stream coverage. Bounded structured failure logs include discovery/dial/session stage, consecutive failure count and a reason capped at 512 characters. Peer closes include code/reason, and policy denial stops without retry.

Queues and wire frames are bounded. Backpressure propagates to sockets instead of dropping accepted messages silently; sustained overload can still lose upstream data and is an operational failure mode. Stale subscription recovery is a heuristic: an inactive book can legitimately have no updates. A per-symbol rate monitor also requires three baseline windows with at least 20 updates/window, followed by two windows below 20% of baseline, before a rate-drop refresh. A cooldown and rotating batch selection prevent resubscription storms or starvation. The activity interval controls heuristic maintenance requests, never book validity. Socket heartbeat failure, disconnect, explicit reset or market removal changes validity. Wall-clock exchange-to-receive samples include clock skew and feed batching; they are not one-way network latency measurements. No connection-spamming or source-port experiments are performed.

## Known limitations

Pre-book buffering deliberately fails the physical source closed on either limit (256 changes for one asset, or 4 MiB accounted storage per socket). This invalidates that socket's other books and requires its paced re-ramp; the current operational reason is `invalid_frame`. An asset-local overflow quarantine would reduce the blast radius, but is not implemented or measured at broad scale. Buffered initialization changes are stably sorted by exchange timestamp, unlike initialized-flow regressions that quarantine; this normalizes pre-book reordering and cannot establish historical continuity. Equal-timestamp arrival order is retained.

A refresh Reset is ordered before unsubscribe. If the eight-entry source notice mailbox is full, that send can wait while its global subscription grant is reserved, temporarily delaying other shards' subscription work until the engine drains. Socket ingestion/output backpressure therefore has a remaining fairness cost; no hard progress bound under a blocked consumer is claimed.
